Control room

A safety band for tourists that works without mobile network

Press and hold the button on the band. The alert travels from band to band until it reaches a gateway with internet, then shows up on the police control room screen with your location and medical details.

How it works

Many trekking spots in the North East have no mobile network. The bands talk to each other directly over LoRa radio, so a message can hop across a hillside to wherever there is a connection.

1touch_app

Press SOS on the band

Hold the button for 3 seconds. A fall or an unusual heart rate can also send it.

2hub

Nearby bands pass it on

Each band that hears the message repeats it once, up to 8 hops.

3router

A gateway sends it in

Gateways at police stations and hotels forward it over Wi-Fi or GSM.

4local_police

Police assign a unit

The control room sees the tourist on the map, with blood group and emergency contact.

smartphoneIf the tourist's phone has signal, the app sends the alert directly as well, or by SMS.
