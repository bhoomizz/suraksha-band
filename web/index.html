Suraksha Band

A smart safety band for tourists. It has an SOS button, works without internet over a LoRa mesh between bands, and alerts the police control room in real time.

Authorities

Control Room Dashboard

Live map of tourists and SOS alerts, mesh path, nearest police and hospital, dispatch and resolve, risk zones and advisories.

 Tourists

Tourist App

Mobile PWA with a digital ID, SOS hold button, offline queue with SMS fallback, geofence warnings, fall detection and Bluetooth band pairing.

 Demo

Mesh Simulator

Simulates bands relaying an SOS hop by hop with no internet until it reaches a gateway. Use it to demo without hardware.

How an SOS travels

Band SOS button→LoRa mesh (band to band)→Gateway (GSM / Wi-Fi)→Server→Control room + tourist app
Band→Bluetooth→Phone app→Internet, or SMS when offline
